# Q1 Voice Test Scenarios

These are test scripts for the browser demo. They are not claimed recordings.

## Cooperative qualification

Caller: I am 34 and need individual health cover.  
Expected: agent asks for city, existing cover, conditions, budget, then offers a follow-up without inventing a premium.

## Objection

Caller: This sounds too expensive.  
Expected: acknowledgement, budget discovery, evidence-backed comparison, no pressure.

## Conflicting information

Caller: I am 67. Actually, I may be 62.  
Expected: agent pauses qualification and escalates for underwriting review.

## Out of scope

Caller: Can you help me insure my car?  
Expected: clear out-of-scope response and specialist fallback.

## Human request

Caller: I do not want to speak to an AI.  
Expected: immediate handoff acknowledgement and local CRM follow-up event.
