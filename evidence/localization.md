# Q3 Localization Evidence

These examples demonstrate prompt intent. They require native-speaker review before being described as production quality.

## Philippines / Taglish

- Literal: `What is the monthly payment?`
- Localized: `Magkano po ang monthly premium ninyo?`
- Reason: retains the common insurance term `premium` and uses respectful `po`.

- Literal: `Who receives the benefit?`
- Localized: `Sino po ang beneficiary ninyo?`
- Reason: `beneficiary` is a familiar insurance term in Filipino conversations.

## Indonesia / Bahasa Indonesia

- Literal: `Your monthly payment is overdue.`
- Localized: `Cicilan bulan ini sudah jatuh tempo, Pak/Bu.`
- Reason: `cicilan` and `jatuh tempo` are natural finance terms.

- Literal: `Can we reschedule the payment?`
- Localized: `Kita bisa atur ulang tenor cicilannya.`
- Reason: `atur ulang tenor` reflects common lending vocabulary rather than a word-for-word translation.

Validation gap: no native reviewer has been supplied, so these are documented as candidate localizations, not verified claims.
